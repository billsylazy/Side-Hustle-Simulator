# Side Hustle Simulator: Art & Feel Guide

(Converted from the original design PDF, Oct 16 2026, @Supreme Leader.)

A cartoony, bright, chunky visual style in the spirit of popular Roblox simulators.

## Art direction

Every hustle should be recognizable from across the map, so exaggerate shapes and
give each zone its own color.

- **Shapes:** Big, rounded, slightly oversized props. A lemonade stand with a giant
  lemon on the roof, a food truck with a huge burger on top.
- **Materials:** Mostly SmoothPlastic and Neon. Avoid realistic textures; they clash
  with the style.
- **Lighting:** Bright daytime with soft shadows. Bump up Saturation slightly in
  ColorCorrection.

| Zone | Signature color |
|---|---|
| Lemonade Stand | Yellow |
| Dog Walking | Grassy green |
| Sneaker Flipping | Purple / streetwear |
| Car Wash | Aqua blue |
| Food Truck | Orange / red |
| Content Creator | Pink / neon |
| Stock Desk | Green-and-black ticker screens |
| Startup HQ | Sleek white and blue |

## Map layout

Use the classic simulator hub: a central spawn plaza with zones arranged in a ring or
along one main road.

- Each zone sits behind a **paywall gate** showing its price, e.g. "$5,000 to unlock
  Sneaker Street."
- Keep locked zones visible from spawn so players always see what they're working toward.

## UI style

Chunky and bold, matching the art style.

- Thick outlines, rounded buttons, bold fonts like Fredoka One or Luckiest Guy.
- Left side: big stacked buttons for Shop, Workers, Rebirth and Quests.
- Top: cash counter with a bouncing coin icon.
- Money popups ("+$50!") flying off every action, plus coin sounds.

## Feel/juice checklist

The juice is half the fun in this genre; every action should feel rewarding.

- [ ] Cash particles and screen-pop when earning
- [ ] Tween animations on every button (slight bounce on hover and click)
- [ ] Upgrade sparkle effects and a level-up sound
- [ ] Workers waddling around doing the hustle animation in the background
- [ ] Big full-screen celebration on rebirth

## Worker character designs

Workers are cute chibi characters whose props show their rarity; players love
collecting characters with personality.

| Rarity | Prop / look |
|---|---|
| Intern | Holds a coffee cup |
| Employee | Everyday work outfit |
| Manager | Carries a clipboard |
| CEO | Sunglasses and a gold suit |
